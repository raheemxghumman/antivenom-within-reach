    How it works

    1. Decision. One qubit per candidate clinic: 1 = stock antivenom.
    2. Objective. Maximise bites that reach antivenom in time; children's bites weigh more; new fridges cost.
    3. Overlap. A village two clinics reach counts once: 1 − (1−xa)(1−xb). That is what makes greedy imperfect.
    4. Exact Hamiltonian. Coverage becomes Z, ZZ, ZZZ… terms, with no truncation and no extra qubits. Verified equal to brute force on all plans.
    5. QAOA. The XY mixer keeps exactly k clinics on, so every shot is a valid plan; the X mixer needs a penalty.
    6. Honest check. Brute force, greedy and random plans score every result with the same exact objective.
